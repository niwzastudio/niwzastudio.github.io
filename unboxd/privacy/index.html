UNBOXD Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026 · App: UNBOXD (com.niwza.unboxd) · Version 1.3
Short version: UNBOXD has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your game progress stays on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads. Optional purchases ("Remove ads" and coin packs) are handled by Google Play.

Information the app stores on your device

Your progress (level, coins, collected figures, daily streak, settings and whether you bought "Remove ads") is saved locally on your device so you can keep playing. The developer does not receive it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

UNBOXD shows ads served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example for a free hint), and occasional full-screen ads between boxes. To serve, personalise (where allowed) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and app performance and crash diagnostics

This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Ads are restricted to content suitable for a parental-guidance audience.

Your choices: In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings, and you can turn off ads between boxes with the "Remove ads" purchase.

Purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases, the one-time "Remove ads" upgrade and coin packs, are processed by Google Play under Google's terms. The developer never sees your payment details. The app only asks Google Play which purchases you own so it can deliver them. Coins can also be earned by playing and are spent only on X-Rays and box repairs. Collectible figures are earned only by playing and are never sold.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app creates an image of your collectible and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes. The developer never receives it.

Permissions

UNBOXD uses internet access (for ads and purchases), vibration (haptic feedback) and the advertising ID (for ads). It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Children

UNBOXD is intended for players aged 16 and over. It has no chat and no user-generated content.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
