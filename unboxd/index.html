UNBOXD

Every level is a mystery parcel. Spin it, work out which tape is on top, and peel every strip in the right order. Beat the clock, pop the box, and add a new item to your shelf.

  • Easy → brutalOne strip of tape at first, then locks, heavy tape, a clock on every box and boss boxes.
  • So satisfyingReal 3D tape that curls as you peel, crunchy sounds and haptics.
  • Daily dropA special box every day. Keep the streak alive.
